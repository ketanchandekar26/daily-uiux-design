Day 004 UX Specifications

1. Cognitive Psychology

  • Sub-16ms Feedback Loop: Financial calculators often suffer from friction when users have to click a manual "Calculate" button. Real-time slider and input listening reduces cognitive latency to zero.
  • Miller's Law (Chunking Balance): Separating Contributed Principal from Accrued Interest visually demonstrates the non-linear superpower of compound growth.
  • Fitts's Law (Thumb Targets): Currency swap and keypad triggers feature generous targets ($\ge 44 \times 44\text{px}$) with tactile scale feedback.

2. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Full keyboard support: Numbers, operators, Escape, and Enter keys mapped to keypad operations.
ARIA live regions (`aria-live="polite"`) ensure screen readers vocalize conversion changes.
Color contrast exceeding 5.2:1 across all numerical readouts on dark backgrounds.

3. Frontend UX Engineering

• Real-Time Dynamic SVG Curve: Interpolates coordinates smoothly as investment horizon and rates fluctuate.

• Matrix Rate Exchange: Instant O(1) FX rate lookup table with inverted reciprocal calculations.
