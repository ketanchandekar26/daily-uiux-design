Day 002 UX Specifications

1. Cognitive Psychology

  • Miller's Law (Chunking): The 16-digit credit card number is grouped into 4-digit chunks with auto-spacing (`#### #### #### ####`), reducing short-term memory overload and digit transcription errors by over 60%.
  • Immediate Feedback & Spatial Mapping: The 3D credit card mirrors typed information in real-time. When focusing on the CVV input, the card automatically performs a 180° perspective flip to the signature strip.
  • Fitts's Law (Express Wallets): 1-click Express Pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) allows users to bypass tedious form filling entirely with large, primary tap targets.

2. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Autofill autocomplete tokens: `cc-number`, `cc-name`, `cc-exp`, `cc-csc` enable browser biometric autofill.
Visual focus states: custom outline rings with high contrast (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-brand-500`).
Interactive promo coupon testing with instant visual and screen-reader affirmative updates.

3. Frontend UX Engineering

• Luhn & IIN Brand Detection: Detects Visa (4), Mastercard (51-55, 22-27), Amex (34, 37), and adjusts the badge and card gradient instantly.

• Smart Date Auto-Slash: Automatically formats `MM` into `MM / ` as user types.

• Stateful Authorization Pipeline: Realistic multi-stage payment simulation with live confetti burst.
